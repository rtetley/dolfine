# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Yamaha C5, recorded at 48 kHz.

Changes: velocity layer 8 of 16, one sample every minor third from A0 to C8 (as distributed by the Tone.js Piano project). Each sample starts 2 ms before the hammer, is cut to 6 seconds with a 1.5 second fade, and is encoded at 128 kbps MP3 with gapless (LAME) headers.

The app plays them through a short room reverb and a limiter (`src/lib/audio/engine.ts`).
